My plan

Ten minutes a day. Three months. One goal per month.

every day · 10 min
  • Visit your 5 writers. Leave real comments, as many as fit in 10 minutes.
  • Someone replies? Write their name down.
  • Wrote a long comment? That's tomorrow's note.
publishing day
  • Reply to every comment on your post.
sundays · 15 min
  1. What did I actually do?
  2. What moved?
  3. What do I drop next week if my energy is low?
One rule: don't change your bio, name or topic for 3 months.
  • Month 120 real people know your name.
  • Month 2Keep them. Pin your best post to the top of your page, and notice who comments every week. Go lighter over Christmas, but don't stop.
  • Month 3Make one small paid thing from what readers thank you for most. A template, a prompt, a setup. Put it behind the paywall of one post.
  • Day 90Compare your numbers to day 1. Now you can decide what to change.

If it starts to feel like a chore: do less. Keep writing.

Good to know

      
It never comments for you. The point is real people getting to know the real you. That doesn't work if AI does the talking.

      
It reads your writers from your Notion page every morning, so if you swap a writer there, the list follows.
